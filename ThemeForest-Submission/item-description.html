YABO — Restaurant & Cafe Responsive HTML Email Template

YABO is a handcrafted, editorial-grade HTML email newsletter template tailored for artisan bakeries, organic cafes, farm-to-table restaurants, specialty coffee roasters, and culinary hospitality brands. Combining Scandinavian minimalism with California lifestyle editorial pacing, YABO delivers an unforgettable culinary storytelling experience directly to your subscribers' inboxes.

Engineered on a rigid 600px table-based grid with Microsoft Outlook MSO conditional width locks, bulletproof table buttons, and fluid mobile media queries, YABO renders reliably across desktop, webmail, and mobile email clients.

Key Features

  • 8 Re-orderable Modular Sections: Easily mix, match, duplicate, or delete sections to build custom weekly specials, menus, or invitations.
  • Mailchimp Ready: Pre-tagged with 35 unique mc:edit tags and 8 mc:repeatable sections for visual drag-and-drop editing in Mailchimp.
  • 100% Fluid Mobile Responsive: Multi-column layouts smoothly convert into single-column vertical cards on screens ≤ 600px (tested on 320px, 375px, 414px, and 600px).
  • Outlook Desktop Protected: Features MSO 600px table locks, zero-spacing resets, and webfont fallback rules to prevent Times New Roman substitution.
  • Retina & Dark Mode Ready: Includes @2x PNG brand marks and social icons with transparent backgrounds for crisp rendering on high-DPI displays.
  • Bulletproof Table Buttons: Solid hex color backgrounds ensure CTA buttons remain fully clickable across Outlook and older clients.
  • Lightweight & Fast: Production HTML is under 45 KB (well below Gmail's 102 KB clipping threshold).
  • Comprehensive Documentation: Detailed 14-section HTML guide covering text editing, image replacement, HTTPS hosting, and ESP deployment.

8 Modular Sections Included

  1. Minimal Brand Header: Clean Retina logo + 3-link editorial navigation.
  2. Editorial Hero Section: Full-width overhead flatlay banner + floating content card with CTA button.
  3. Philosophy Story Intro: Botanical accent glyph, editorial serif headline, and philosophy copy.
  4. Signature Dishes Zigzag: 4 alternating dish rows with circular photography, category eyebrows, and descriptions on Sage Green.
  5. Chef's Favourite Spotlight: Gold star accents, large circular plate photo, pricing, and primary order CTA.
  6. Dine-in Sanctuary: Walk-in invitation copy with opening hours pill badge.
  7. Takeout & Delivery Banner: Packaging photo + generous green content strip with partner badges (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Postmates).
  8. Minimalist Footer: Footer brand mark, secondary navigation, social icons, physical address, and Mailchimp unsubscribe tags.

Client Rendering Architecture & Compatibility

Engineered with defensive table markup, MSO conditional comments, and bulletproof fallbacks for major rendering engines:

  • Apple Mail (macOS & iOS)
  • Gmail (Webmail, iOS App, Android App)
  • Microsoft Outlook (Office 365, 2016, 2019, 2021 Windows & Mac)
  • Outlook.com & Windows Mail
  • Yahoo! Mail & AOL Mail
  • Mozilla Thunderbird
  • Mobile browsers and native clients (iPhone 12/13/14/15, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel)
